Frequently asked questions

A few important details.

Is NarrowBid part of the UK government?

No. NarrowBid is an independent prototype. It makes a curated view of public information from the official Find a Tender service. Always confirm the source notice.

Will I automatically qualify for the tenders shown?

No. Keyword and service matches only indicate topical relevance. The buyer may require particular turnover, security vetting, experience or professional credentials that NarrowBid cannot verify.

Is it free?

The opportunity explorer and initial fit checking in this prototype are free. £19/month is only a proposed future subscription price. No checkout is currently offered.

How fresh is the tender data?

The feed is checked daily. The actual last-sync timestamp is shown in the explorer. If refreshing fails, we make that visible rather than pretending it is current.

Can I suggest a missing feature?

Yes. You can share a feature suggestion on GitHub. Suggestions are public, so please keep personal and client details out of your post.
